1974 Observation Records

The following entries were recovered from photographic logs, handwritten observation sheets, and partial typed transcripts.

Observation 74-031

Date: March 12, 1974
Time: 22:41–23:56 EST
Conditions: Clear / low atmospheric distortion

Routine photographic survey completed. Polaris and surrounding reference stars recorded within expected positional tolerance.

No significant optical deviation observed.

Observation 74-039

Date: March 28, 1974
Time: 01:32–02:06 EST
Conditions: Clear / light wind

Minor light artifact observed approximately 2.3 degrees from expected reference position.

Initial review suggests contamination of photographic plate. Secondary plate requested for comparison.

Observation 74-041

Date: April 3, 1974
Time: 00:58–02:19 EST
Conditions: Clear

Previously recorded artifact appears on secondary photographic plate.

Darkroom contamination considered unlikely. Telescope optics inspected following observation. No surface defect identified.

Observation 74-044

Date: April 7, 1974
Time: 01:47–03:08 EST
Conditions: Clear / stable atmosphere

Unidentified light source remains visible within survey area.

Comparison against available star charts produced no corresponding object.

Additional photographic exposures requested.

Observation 74-047

Date: April 11, 1974
Time: 01:55–02:26 EST
Conditions: Clear

Object visible immediately following initial alignment.

At 02:13 EST, telescope repositioned approximately four degrees west for equipment verification.

Object remained centered in frame.

Alignment procedure repeated.

Same result.
