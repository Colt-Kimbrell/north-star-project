Project Summary

The North Star Project was an independent astronomical research initiative active between 1972 and 1977.

The project was established to conduct long-duration observations of fixed stellar reference points in the northern sky, with particular emphasis on Polaris and surrounding star fields.

Researchers were primarily concerned with measuring atmospheric distortion, optical drift, long-exposure imaging variation, and apparent changes in stellar position across repeated observations.

The project operated from a privately maintained research facility located in a rural area of the eastern United States.

Research Objectives

  • Conduct repeated photographic surveys of Polaris and nearby stellar fields.
  • Measure long-term optical variation across standardized observation periods.
  • Compare photographic negatives across multiple seasons and atmospheric conditions.
  • Record instrumental drift and telescope alignment data.
  • Maintain an independent observational archive for later comparison and analysis.

Research Facility

The North Star Project operated from a small observatory complex constructed between late 1971 and early 1972.

The facility included a primary observation dome, photographic development room, equipment storage area, administrative office, and an underground records room used for archival storage.

Surviving documents indicate that the observatory maintained continuous nighttime staffing during several extended observation periods between 1973 and 1975.

Facility diagrams and equipment inventories remain incomplete.

Project Chronology

Date Event
1971 Planning and construction of the research facility begins.
1972 Formal observation program begins.
1973 Long-duration photographic surveys expanded.
1974 Increased observation frequency and additional overnight staffing recorded.
1975 Portions of the observation program temporarily suspended for equipment review.
1976 Limited observation activity resumes.
1977 Project formally discontinued.

Archival Note

A complete administrative record of the North Star Project has not been recovered.

Existing documents contain several gaps in project chronology, including incomplete personnel records and missing observation materials from portions of 1974 and 1975.

These absences were documented during the 2003–2004 digitization initiative and are believed to predate the transfer of the archive to digital storage.
