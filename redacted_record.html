SUBJECT:
OBSERVATION PROCEDURE REVISION

--------------------------------------------------

Effective immediately, all overnight observation activity
involving the northern reference field is to cease no later
than 02:10 EST.

No optical realignment is to be performed between:

    02:10 EST — 02:20 EST

Personnel are not to attempt manual correction of tracking
deviation during this interval.

Under no circumstances should the primary telescope be
re-centered after 02:14.

--------------------------------------------------

The events recorded on April 11 and April 12 are not to be
included in routine observational summaries.

Photographic plates:

    NSP-74-047
    NSP-74-048
    ███████████

are to be removed from general catalog access.

Any duplicate exposures are to be destroyed.

No additional copies are to be made.

--------------------------------------------------

Personnel are instructed not to discuss the April observation
sequence outside the project facility.

Dr. Miriam Vale has requested suspension of continued
observation.

Request denied.

--------------------------------------------------

    █████████████████████████████████████
