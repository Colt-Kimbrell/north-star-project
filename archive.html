About This Archive

The North Star Project Digital Archive contains surviving documentation from an independent astronomical observation program conducted between 1972 and 1977.

Materials include observation logs, equipment records, photographic negatives, personnel documentation, handwritten research notes, and audio recordings produced during the project's operation.

The surviving materials were transferred to digital formats between 2003 and 2004 following deterioration of portions of the original collection.

Several source materials were incomplete or damaged prior to digitization. Users may encounter missing pages, incomplete photographs, transcription errors, or corrupted files.

Digitization Notes

Original materials were cataloged and digitized beginning September 2003.

The digitization project was suspended in August 2004. No additional materials were processed after that date.

Approximately 13% of indexed archive materials could not be successfully recovered.
